OpenBeach 3.0.3
- The app opens on Home: Continue resumes the match in one tap, a reload goes back into it, 15 minutes in the background goes to Home.
- A match closed on the server opens read-only and keeps no sync error.
- Restoring a backup no longer deletes the other matches on the device.
- The match-end and set-end files can be restored.
- The last backup is saved before the app closes.
- "Auto-download data at set end" is remembered.
