OpenBeach 2.0.0, the first Android release.
- Scores beach volleyball matches offline, on the tablet.
- Venue mode: connect to a local server (Options, Server) and open the referee or livescore view on a second tablet.
- Cloud sync with backend.openvolley.app for signed-in scorers.
- The scoreboard locks to landscape; the other screens also work in portrait.
- Back asks before closing the app.
